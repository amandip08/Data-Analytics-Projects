# # Car Sales Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze car sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

## Objective

To visualize sales performance, revenue trends, and customer purchasing patterns through interactive dashboards and KPIs.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Excel

## Dashboard Features

* Total Sales Analysis
* Revenue Tracking
* Vehicle Category Performance
* Monthly Sales Trends
* Interactive Filters and Slicers
* KPI Monitoring

## Business Benefits

* Supports strategic decision making
* Identifies top-performing products
* Tracks sales growth patterns
* Improves business reporting efficiency

## Key Insights

* Revenue performance across different categories
* Sales trends over time
* Product-wise sales comparison
* Business growth indicators

## Future Enhancements

* Predictive Sales Forecasting
* Customer Segmentation Analysis
* Real-Time Dashboard Integration
* Advanced KPI Monitoring
